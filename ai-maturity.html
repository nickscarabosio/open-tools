AI Organizational Maturity

AI maturity is not a technology ladder. It measures how much of an organization has reorganized around AI — from one person’s disposition to a redesigned operating model. Each stage converts more human capability into productive capacity.

The spine: human capability → productive capacity
L0
Openness
Am I open to the possibility that AI changes how I work?

The precondition for everyone. This is disposition, not deployment. The only human-centered rung — different in kind.

Closed signals: avoidance & delay, “that’s not for my role”, dismissing output after one bad attempt, quiet refusal to change how work gets done.

A closed Level 0 blocks every level above it.

L1
Individual Productivity
How can AI help me do my work faster?

Mainstream today. Existing work, done faster. Tools: Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot. Work: drafting, summarizing, document creation, code completion. Typical gain: 20–30% personal productivity.

The trap: mistaking adoption for institutionalization. Institutionalized Level 1 has standardized prompts, clear expectations, governance, and measured workflows.

L2
Workflow Redesign
How should this workflow change because AI exists?

The hard stage — organizational change, not technology. Whole processes (hiring, onboarding, sales, finance, customer service) are rebuilt around AI before agents are deployed.

Failure mode: inserting AI into a process without changing the process. Same shape = same speed.

This is the stage most organizations skip — and the one that makes Level 3 pay off.

L3
AI Operating Teams
What work should AI permanently own?

Each person operates with a persistent AI operating team — teammates, not agents. 10–50 teammates depending on role complexity.

Examples: Sales (Pipeline AI, Proposal AI, CRM AI, Meeting AI), Finance (AP AI, Forecast AI, Cash Flow AI, Contract AI), Marketing (Research AI, Content AI, Analytics AI).

Work happens continuously rather than in bursts.

L4
AI-Native Organization
If AI existed from day one, how would we build this company differently?

The organization itself is redesigned. AI is built in parallel until it can run the operation.

Characteristics: org chart and decision rights redesigned • AI owns execution, humans own judgment • dramatically increased spans of control • fewer management layers • teams organized around outcomes, not departments.

Within ten years, many startups will be AI-native by default. Enterprise incumbents will lag dramatically.

The Trap
Most companies jump from Level 1 to Level 3 and skip Level 2 because vendors sell packaged “AI employees” before workflows are redesigned. The result: they automate a broken process and get disappointing ROI. Level 2 is what makes Level 3 pay off.
